§ Regulatory03 / 05
  
 
REG
Finance Canada · OSFI
Canada: draft Stablecoin Regulations; OSFI effective 1 Nov
Finance Canada's draft (Canada Gazette Part I, 24 Oct) requires Bank of Canada registration, 1:1 high-quality liquid reserves and at-par redemption. It bans yield, excludes banks, trust companies and other prudentially regulated firms from the issuer regime, and has a 60-day comment period. [16][17] OSFI's crypto capital and liquidity guideline applies to RBC from 1 Nov. [18] There was no in-window CSA, CIRO or Bank of Canada item [Data gap].
 
→ SO WHAT FOR CAPITAL MARKETS
Reserve custody for non-bank issuers is a Global Transaction Banking deposit opportunity. Capital Markets should feed the comment letter and encode OSFI's Group 1 and Group 2 rules in the Watchtower now.
 
STABLECOINS
OCC · SEC
US: OCC GENIUS final rule; SEC exam priorities
The OCC's final rule (new 12 CFR 15) covers reserves, redemption, custody, capital and foreign issuers, effective 18 Jan 2027. Existing issuers with a substantially complete application may seek up to 12 months of waivers. [13][14][15] The SEC's FY2027 exam priorities add tokenization controls at clearing agencies and Reg SCI reviews of tokenized securities and extended hours (23 Oct). [19]
 
→ SO WHAT FOR CAPITAL MARKETS
Our US broker-dealer and DTCC-facing tokenization will be examined on controls, so build the evidence into the Watchtower and the intraday core from day one.

UK/EU and standard setters
